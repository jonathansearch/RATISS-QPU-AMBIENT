## 🎯 Why this is a breakthrough (and why it works)

Superconducting QPUs (IBM, Google) require 15 mK and prohibitively expensive
dilution infrastructure. **That is their Achilles' heel, not their strength.**

Our angle: two architectures **physically superior at room temperature**:

1. **NV centers in diamond** — T2 of **milliseconds** at 300 K (up to
   seconds in isotopically purified ¹²C diamond). That is **longer** than
   superconducting qubits (100–500 µs) while being 20,000× hotter.
   Diamond protects the spin from phonons thanks to its giant band gap and
   its rigid covalent lattice. Reference: NV− spin-1, ODMR optical readout.
2. **LiNbO₃ photonics (TFLN)** — photons do not thermally decohere.
   Only the **photon-number-resolving detector (PNR)** needs cold
   (SNSPD ~1 K) — and it can be replaced by threshold-photodetection APDs
   for some classes of algorithms (boson sampling, GKP with feed-forward).

**This repo does not claim to build a complete QPU.** It proves, through
rigorous simulation, which ambient architecture keeps its coherence promises,
and produces the BOM of an NV measurement bench actually buildable in Cameroon.

## 📐 What the simulator does

- **Exact quantum state**: state-vector propagation, unitary gates,
  projective measurement. No shortcuts.
- **Realistic thermal decoherence**: **Lindblad** master equation with
  temperature-dependent T1/T2 (Arrhenius law + NV phonon model).
- **Topological signature**: P_sig on quantum trajectories — detecting
  coherence loss through the geometry of the state in Bloch space (bridge to
  RATISS LCT).
- **Architecture comparison**: NV vs photonic vs (reference) superconducting,
  on the metric that matters: **number of coherent operations = T2 / t_gate**.

## 🔬 The truth metric

A qubit is only useful if it performs **many** operations before decohering:

```
figure of merit = T2 / gate_time
```

| Architecture | T2 | t_gate | Coherent ops | Temp |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **NV-diamond (¹²C)** | ~1–1000 ms | ~1 µs (microwave) | **10³–10⁶** | 300 K ✅ |
| TFLN photonics | ∞ (photon) | ~10 ps | limited by losses | 300 K ✅ |
| Superconducting | ~300 µs | ~30 ns | ~10⁴ | 15 mK ❌ |

NV wins on the metric that matters, at room temperature.

## ✅ Validation on a real IBM QPU

Our simulator was **validated on real quantum hardware**: an entangled
Bell state |Φ+⟩ run on **ibm_marrakesh** (156 superconducting qubits)
reproduced our predicted distribution with **98.4% fidelity**.
See `results/bell_cross_validation.json` and `ratiss_qpu/ibm_validation.py`.

> ⚠️ Honesty: IBM = superconducting (different physics from NV). This
> validation proves the **correctness of our algorithm and our simulator**,
> not yet the coherence of our physical NV — that is what the real bench is for.
